# Product

## Register

brand

## Platform

web

## Users

People looking up the company: a partner, a reader, or someone who needs a person at Aivora Labs. The company is one legal entity. Products sit under it, each with its own name. Animaay is the product that can be named today. Do not invent the others.

## Product Purpose

The company page for Aivora Labs. One company, multiple products underneath. Success is a visitor who sees that structure, can open a product, and can email the company. The page does not stand in for a product site.

## Positioning

Aivora Labs is an AI lab. It builds products for everyday use. Each product keeps its own name, site, and visual theme. Animaay is the one that can be named today.

## Conversion & proof

- Primary CTA: open a product (today, See Animaay). Secondary: email info@aivoralabs.si.
- The line a visitor remembers after 10 seconds: Aivora Labs is the company. The products keep their own names.
- Belief ladder: this is the company, each product is separate, the email reaches the company.
- Proof on hand: none supplied. Do not invent testimonials, counts, press, a team, or an address.

## Brand Personality

Precise, inked, cinematic. A film laboratory's colophon: the quiet card after the picture, with one terracotta seal. Confident and plain. The only public contact is info@aivoralabs.si. There are no social accounts.

## Anti-references

A clone of the Animaay product site. A SaaS page of identical icon cards, metric heroes, and a row of social icons. Cream agency templates. Startup language that claims a movement. Anything that invents a team, an office, or a number we do not have.

## Design Principles

1. The first line says who we are.
2. Describe only what Animaay actually does.
3. The email is the ending, and the only contact.
4. Vermillion belongs to the studio and the product; the field around it stays black.
5. Leave out proof we do not have.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Body text at least 4.5:1, large text at least 3:1. Visible focus, a skip link, and a reduced-motion path that leaves every element on screen. No extra audience requirement was specified.
